🧠 LLMCogKit — Personalized AI, Without the Prompt Hassle

LLMCogKit is an open-source toolkit designed to make your AI assistant feel truly yours. Whether you're a busy professional, a curious newcomer, or part of a team standardizing workflows, LLMCogKit helps you build a reusable, structured profile.md file that conditions large language models (LLMs) to align with your tone, tools, and tasks — across any platform.

Instead of rewriting prompts or tweaking settings every time, LLMCogKit walks you through a conversational onboarding experience to capture your style, preferences, and goals. It then generates modular files that ensure predictable, personalized AI behavior—from memory and formatting to assistant personas.

✨ Imagine LLMs that actually remember what matters to you, speak your language, and support your daily routines — without constant reprogramming.
